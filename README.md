# Pharmacy Education Guide

This repository contains comprehensive information about pharmacy courses and career paths.

## Contents

- **[Pharmacy Course Guide](Pharmacy-Course-Guide.md)** - Detailed guide covering the best pharmacy courses, degree types, specializations, and career advice

## About

This guide was created to help students and parents make informed decisions about pharmacy education. It covers everything from undergraduate pharmacy programs to specialized doctoral degrees, career prospects, and tips for success in the pharmaceutical field.

## Quick Overview

Pharmacy offers excellent career opportunities in:
- Clinical practice and patient care
- Pharmaceutical research and development
- Hospital and community pharmacy
- Regulatory affairs and industry roles

The guide provides detailed information about prerequisites, course selection, and career pathways to help you choose the best pharmacy education path.
